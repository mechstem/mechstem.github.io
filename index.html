About MechStem

Research-led engineering with real-world impact.

Founded in 2017, MechStem began as a technical initiative focused on deep engineering fundamentals across software, electronics, and mechanical systems.

Today, MechStem operates as an independent research lab, conducting applied research, building experimental platforms, and collaborating with industry, academia, and engineers worldwide.

Our philosophy emphasizes rigor, system-level thinking, and long-term engineering value over short-term trends.
