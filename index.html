Research Domains

We focus on building reliable, scalable, and intelligent engineering systems — from low-level hardware to cyber-physical platforms.

Embedded Systems & Platform Engineering

Embedded Linux, RTOS, BSP, device drivers, real-time systems, camera and sensor integration for production-grade platforms.

Edge AI & Intelligent Systems

On-device machine learning, computer vision, sensor fusion, and edge–cloud architectures for real-time intelligence.

Mechanical & Cyber-Physical Systems

Machine design, robotics, simulation, and tightly integrated mechatronic systems with embedded control.

About MechStem

Founded in 2017, MechStem is an independent engineering research lab focused on building intelligent, production-grade systems.

Our work bridges embedded platforms, AI-driven perception, and mechanical system design — with a long-term vision to build systems that endure, scale, and matter.
